🍋 Beyond Work ⌄

Lemonade Lovers

One of the experiences closest to my heart has been co-founding Lemonade Lovers, a community fundraising initiative supporting SickKids Foundation.

What began as a way to give back has grown into something much more meaningful. Through fundraising events, community outreach, and the support of volunteers, we've raised more than $135,000 for children's healthcare.

The experience has reminded me how much people can accomplish when they come together around a shared purpose.
